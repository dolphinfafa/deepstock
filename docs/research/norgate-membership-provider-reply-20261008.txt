ALLMARKETDAYS evaluates historical constituent status even when the stock didn't trade that day.
Yes.
The Index Constituent Timeseries is tied to the price time series.  You can use the padding you desire.  No announcement dates are provided - only effective dates are indicated.  OTC transitions are handled by Major Exchange Listing timeseries in the same manner.
Missing days just means there were no last-sale-eligible trades for that date.  If you want to use padding, you can do so.
