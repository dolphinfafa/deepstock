## Strategy Research Implementation

- Implemented a reproducible defensive ETF backtest for `SPY`, `QQQ`, `IWM`,
  `TLT`, `IEF`, `GLD`, and defensive asset `SHY`.
- Signals require positive 12-month momentum and a close above the 200-day
  moving average. Eligible assets receive inverse-volatility weights, subject
  to 80% total exposure and 20% single-position caps; residual weight goes to
  `SHY`.
- Targets are calculated at month end and shifted one trading day before
  returns are applied. The backtest includes configurable transaction costs.
- Added CSV data validation, a CLI that writes reproducible artifacts, and unit
  tests. The implementation does not connect to TWS or contain order code.
