## Fixed-Split Robustness Validation

- Added a segmented backtest path. The split date is the first out-of-sample
  trading day; the out-of-sample segment is rebased for reporting but retains
  only weights generated causally from available prior history.
- Added `scripts/run_defensive_etf_robustness.py`. It records the exact input
  coverage, split, configuration grid, in-sample and out-of-sample metrics, and
  an explicit rule that ranks only by in-sample Sharpe ratio.
- On the laptop's local Massive total-return dataset, ran all 18 combinations:
  150/200/250-day trend, 126/252-day momentum, and 42/63/126-day volatility.
  The split was fixed at 2024-08-21: 753 in-sample sessions from 2021-08-23 to
  2024-08-20 and 501 out-of-sample sessions through 2026-08-20.
- Out-of-sample strategy returns ranged from 14.14% to 19.22%; annualized
  volatility ranged from 6.70% to 7.48%, Sharpe from 0.96 to 1.35, maximum
  drawdown from -5.12% to -4.42%, turnover from 2.63 to 4.45, and modeled costs
  from 0.26% to 0.44% of initial capital. `SPY` returned 39.72% in the same
  out-of-sample period.
- The highest in-sample-ranked configuration was 252-day momentum, 250-day
  trend, and 42-day volatility. Its out-of-sample total return was 19.22%,
  Sharpe 1.35, and maximum drawdown -4.55%. No parameter was selected using
  out-of-sample results.
- Conclusion: the narrow grid supports a stable defensive, low-drawdown
  profile, but does not support paper-order implementation because every tested
  configuration materially underperformed `SPY` over the out-of-sample period.
